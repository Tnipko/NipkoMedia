Nezdor FAQ

Nezdor is designed to be playable without hand-holding. This page covers the “okay, but how do I…?” basics.

Platform & System

Does the game require internet access?

No. All gameplay systems are designed to function fully offline once installed.

Does the game include chat or external communication?

No. Nezdor is a single-player experience and does not include chat, messaging, or any form of external communication.

Is progress saved locally?

Yes. All game progress is saved directly on the player’s device, allowing continued play without an internet connection.

Gameplay

How do I save my game?

Use the in-game save option from the Inventory (treasure chest icon). Selecting Save Game will save your progress. Choosing Exit Game will also save and return you to the main menu.

I looted items — where do they go?

Looted items are placed in your Inventory (treasure chest icon). If your inventory is full, items will drop to the ground in a loot bag. Once space is available, you can retrieve the contents.

How do I equip weapons and armor?

Open your Inventory, select a weapon or armor item, and drag it into the appropriate equipment slot.

How does crafting work?

Crafting requires a recipe or pattern, materials, and sometimes a tool. Recipes and tools are purchased from merchants. Materials are gathered through exploration.

Crafting must be done at the correct station (Workbench, Oven, or Alchemist Lab). Once a recipe is learned, it does not need to be repurchased, though tools and materials are still required.

Where can I store items and coins?

Use the Bank to store excess inventory and manage currency safely.

I finished a quest. Is there more to do?

Most quests lead into follow-up quests. Pay attention to the quest giver’s final dialog for hints. If you miss it, check the Conversation Log for the recorded message.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Email nipkomedia@gmail.com with details about what happened, where you were in the game, and what you expected to occur.
